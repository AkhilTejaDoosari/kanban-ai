# Decision Log

Record only durable choices future developers/agents would reasonably question.

Do not record trivial implementation details.

## ADR template

### ADR-NNN — <title>

**Status:** PROPOSED | ACCEPTED | SUPERSEDED

**Context**

Why a decision was needed.

**Decision**

What was chosen.

**Alternatives considered**

What realistic alternatives were considered.

**Consequences**

Benefits, costs, constraints, and follow-up implications.

**Cost impact**

If the decision adds a paid/usage-metered service, record:
- estimated monthly cost
- budget status
- free alternative considered
- human approval
